3. Interaction narrative

Scenario: a new patient books an appointment with a doctor and then checks their upcoming appointments.

    1. Starting the clinic system. When the clinic opens the program, Main.main() runs. It creates the Booking object, which acts as the receptionist, 
    and calls makeDocs(). This fills the doctors array with the clinic's doctors, each as a Doctor object with its own doctorID, name and an empty timeslots 
    array of 30-minute slots. Main then calls WelcomeMSG(), which greets the patient and offers the choice to book a new appointment or check an existing one.

    2. Registering the patient. The patient chooses to book. Booking.patientnew(id, name, number) takes the details the patient types in and creates a Patient object. 
    Main stores it in its patients array, so the clinic has a record of who has come in.

    3. Choosing a doctor. Booking.chooseDoc() lists the doctors from the doctors array, and the patient picks one. Doctor.displayschedule() then shows the status of 
    that doctor's 30-minute slots, so the patient can see which times are open and which are already taken.

    4. Creating the appointment. The patient picks a free time. Booking.appointmentnew(doc, pat, appid, time) creates an Appointment object that links the doctor's doctorID, 
    the patient's health card number and the chosen time. Main adds it to its appointments array.

    5. Booking the slot. Booking.booktime(timeindex, appointment, doc) checks the chosen index in that doctor's timeslots array. If the slot is free, it stores 
    the Appointment there, so the doctor's schedule now shows that time as taken and no other patient can book it. If the slot is already taken, nothing is stored and 
    the patient is asked to choose another time.

    5. Checking an existing appointment. Later, the patient returns and chooses to check their appointments. Booking.checkapps(doc, pat) goes through the doctor's timeslots, 
    finds the appointments whose health card number matches the patient's, and displays them. It reads the details through Appointment.getdoctorID() and Appointment.gettime(), 
    and the patient through Patient.getName().
